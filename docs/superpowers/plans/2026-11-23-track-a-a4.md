## Scope and derivation

Change only `app/shaders/lines/hull.vert` in production. The existing pipeline already
does front-face depth prepass, expanded back-face rendering, and line-mask composition.
Transform the normal with `normalMatrix`, then project its direction. For clip position
`c` and projected normal `n`, the derivative of `c.xy/c.w` is proportional to
`n.xy*c.w - c.xy*n.w`. Normalize this direction in **pixel coordinates**, then convert
the requested pixel displacement back to clip xy. Preserve clip z/w. Guard zero
directions, nonpositive width/resolution, and positions on/behind the camera.
This is independently derived from the perspective quotient and the starter contract.
